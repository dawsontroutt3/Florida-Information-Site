Fun Facts

  • Miami's nickname is "The Magic City," a reference to its rapid growth and development in the early 20th century.
  • Miami is noted as the only major city in the United States founded by a woman, Julia Tuttle.
  • When World War II began, Miami became a base for U.S. defense against German submarines due to its prime location on the southern coast of Florida.
