About Lakeland

Lakeland is a city in Polk County, Florida, known for its lakes and parks. It is the central city of the Lakeland metropolitan area.

Places to Visit

  1. Lake Mirror: A beautiful lake right in the middle of downtown with all the food and shopping.
  2. Joker Marchant Stadium: Home field to the Lakeland Flying Tigers, the minor league affiliate of the Detroit Tigers.
  3. The Polk Theatre: A historic theater in the heart of downtown. It has hosted various legends over the years, and still provides a wonderful theater experience.

Fun Facts

  • Lakeland's nickname and mascot the swan originates back to 1957, when Queen Elizabeth II gifted two royal swans to the city. The many swans around the city present day are descendants of those original swans.
  • “Edward Scissorhands” was filmed at Southgate Shopping Center, a shopping mall in the middle of town.
  • Florida Southern College, located in Lakeland, was designed by Frank Lloyd Wright. The campus is his largest collection of architecture in the world!
